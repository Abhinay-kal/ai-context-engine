# Product Context
- **Name:** AI Context Engine (WP AI Context)
- **Description:** A WordPress plugin that exports your site's configuration (environment details, active plugins, themes, and settings) into an AI-friendly format (JSON/Markdown) while redacting sensitive secrets.
- **Value Proposition:** Reduces AI hallucinations by providing exact context, ending endless cycles of manual screenshotting and copy-pasting.
- **Key Features:** Collectors, Adapters, Privacy Filters, Exporters.
